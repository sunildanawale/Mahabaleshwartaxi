# Google Search Console Setup Checklist 🚀

Now that your website is technically optimized for SEO, follow these steps to start ranking on Page-1:

### 1. Verify Ownership
- Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/).
- Add a new property: `https://sunildanawale.github.io/Mahabaleshwartaxi/`.
- **GitHub Pages Method**: Choose the "HTML tag" verification method. Copy the meta tag and paste it into the `<head>` of your `index.html`.

### 2. Submit Your Sitemap
- In the left sidebar, click on **Sitemaps**.
- Enter `sitemap.xml` in the "Add a new sitemap" field and click **Submit**.
- This tells Google exactly which pages to index first.

### 3. Check Mobile Usability
- After indexing (usually 2–3 days), check the **Mobile Usability** report.
- Ensure there are no "Text too small" or "Clickable elements too close" errors (I've already optimized for this, but Google's confirmation is key).

### 4. Monitor Search Queries
- Look at the **Performance** report to see which keywords are bringing people to your site.
- If you notice you're ranking for "Satara taxi" but not "Mahabaleshwar taxi," we can adjust the content slightly to compensate.

### 5. Local Citation Strategy (The Next Step)
To rank in the "Maps Pack" (the box with 3 results and a map):
1. **Google Business Profile**: Create/Claim yours if you haven't. Use the SAME phone number and address as in the website footer.
2. **Reviews**: Ask happy customers to review you on Google Maps. Mentioning "taxi in Mahabaleshwar" in their review helps a lot!
