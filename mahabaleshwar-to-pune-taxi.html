Mahabaleshwar to Pune Return FAQs

How many hours before my flight at Pune Airport should I leave Mahabaleshwar?

We advise leaving at least 4.5 to 5 hours prior to your flight departure time. The descent via Pasarni Ghat takes ~40 minutes, highway driving via Shirwal takes ~90 minutes, and crossing Pune city bypass traffic near Chandani Chowk / Katraj can take 45-60 minutes during peak hours.

Will the cab pick me up right from my resort lobby in Mahabaleshwar?

Yes, our driver arrives directly at your hotel or resort reception at the requested time to assist with your luggage loading.

Can we stop to buy fresh strawberries and chikki on the return drive?

Certainly. Your driver will pause at authentic strawberry farm shops along the Panchgani-Wai highway so you can purchase farm-fresh boxes without rushing.

How do I pay for my Mahabaleshwar to Pune cab?

You can pay the driver via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or cash directly upon safe arrival at your Pune drop destination.
