Mahabaleshwar to Mumbai Return FAQs

How much buffer time should I keep for a flight departing from Mumbai Airport (BOM)?

We recommend starting at least 7 hours prior to your scheduled flight departure. Driving time is typically 5.5 to 6.5 hours depending on traffic at the Vashi creek bridge and Western Express Highway.

Can the driver drop us at two nearby addresses in Mumbai?

Yes, if the two addresses are along the same travel corridor (e.g. dropping luggage at Dadar and proceeding to Bandra), it can be easily coordinated with prior notification.

Can we safely carry fresh strawberries back to Mumbai?

Yes. We advise packing strawberry boxes in aerated crates. Our ventilated vehicle trunks keep the fruit fresh and well-protected throughout the highway drive.

How can I confirm my return taxi from Mahabaleshwar to Mumbai?
