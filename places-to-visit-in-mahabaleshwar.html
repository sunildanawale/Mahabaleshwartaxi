Category 1

Iconic Mahabaleshwar Viewpoints

Majestic valley overlooks, anti-gravity cliffs, and panoramic vantage points.

Arthur's Seat (Queen of Points)

Tour 1

Perched at 1,470m altitude, Arthur's Seat commands stunning views of the Savitri Valley and Bramha Aranya. Famous for its anti-gravity phenomenon where lightweight items float upward. The circuit covers Window Point, Tiger Spring, Hunter Point, Echo Point, and Malcolm Point.

Included in: Tour 1 (Mahabaleshwar Darshan)
Tour Duration: 3 Hours 30 Minutes
Tour Tariff: ₹1,200/- (4-Seater Tourist Taxi)
Entry Fee: Free (Point Parking extra ₹30-50)
Guide

Kate's Point & Elephant's Head

Tour 1

Located at 1,290m overlooking Krishna Valley and Dhom Dam. Features the world-famous natural rock overhand resembling an elephant's head and trunk (Needle Hole Point) alongside Echo Point.

Included in: Tour 1 (Mahabaleshwar Darshan)
Tour Duration: 3 Hours 30 Minutes
Tour Tariff: ₹1,200/- (4-Seater Tourist Taxi)
Entry Fee: Free (Point Parking extra)
Guide

Wilson Point (Sunrise Point)

Tour 2

The highest point in Mahabaleshwar at 1,439m (Sindola Hill). Features three masonry observation towers offering 360-degree views and is the premier spot for early morning sunrise viewing.

Included in: Tour 2 (or Standalone Sunrise Drop: ₹400 / Return: ₹600)
Best Time: 5:45 AM – 6:30 AM (Sunrise)
Tour Tariff: ₹1,200/- (Tour 2 Complete Circuit)
Guide

Bombay Point & Lodwick Point

Tour 2

Situated on the old road to Mumbai, Bombay Point is the classic evening hotspot for breathtaking Sahyadri sunsets. Nearby Lodwick Point features the historic 25-ft General Lodwick memorial obelisk.

Included in: Tour 2 (or Standalone Sunset Drop: ₹400 / Return: ₹600)
Best Time: 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM (Sunset)
Tour Tariff: ₹1,200/- (Tour 2 Complete Circuit)
Guide
Category 2

Waterfalls & Scenic Lakes

Plunging 600ft cascades, serene reservoir waters, and peaceful boating lakes.

Lingmala Waterfall

Tour 2

Located 6 km from town near the forest bungalow, this majestic 600-foot waterfall cascades into the Venna Valley. Features a lower safe pool and an upper viewpoint trek with mist and lush greenery.

Included in: Tour 2 (Waterfall & Sunset Circuit)
Best Season: July to December (Peak monsoon flow)
Tour Tariff: ₹1,200/- (4-Seater Taxi, 3.5 Hrs)
Forest Entry: Approx ₹20 per person (Paid at gate)
Guide

Venna Lake & Mini Dam

Tour 2 / Local Drop

Built in 1842 by Appasaheb Maharaj over 28 sprawling acres. The central recreational hub for paddle and row boating, horse riding, and lakeside street food (fresh strawberries, corn, chana).

Included in: Tour 2 (Venna Mini Dam) or Direct Taxi Drop
Timings: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM
Local Drop Tariff: ₹400 Drop / ₹600 Return
Guide
Category 3

Sacred Temples of Old Mahabaleshwar

1,000-year-old Hemadpanthi stone temples, origins of 5 sacred rivers, and spiritual peace.

Panchganga & Lord Shiva Temple

Tour 1

13th-century Yadava-era shrine at Kshetra Mahabaleshwar where five holy rivers (Krishna, Venna, Koyna, Savitri, Gayatri) emerge through a stone Gomukh spout. Adjacent Mahabaleshwar Temple houses the sacred Atma Lingam.

Included in: Tour 1 (Mahabaleshwar Darshan)
Temple Timings: 5:00 AM – 12:00 PM & 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM
Tour Tariff: ₹1,200/- (Tour 1) or ₹400 Drop / ₹600 Return

Krishnabai Temple

Tour 1

A short 5-minute walk behind Panchganga Temple leads to this hidden stone sanctuary overlooking the majestic Krishna Valley. Featuring ancient carved stone pillars and a tranquil holy pool.

Included in: Tour 1 (Covered alongside Panchganga Temple)
Entry Fee: Free
Tour Tariff: ₹1,200/- (Tour 1 Complete 3.5 Hrs)
Category 4

Historic Forts & Maratha Heritage

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's fortress of valor, battlegrounds, and royal temples.

Pratapgad Fort (Tour 3)

Tour 3

Constructed in 1656 by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, Pratapgad is renowned for the historic Battle of Pratapgad against Afzal Khan. Features the sacred Bhavani Mata Temple, 17-ft Shivaji Maharaj bronze statue, Kadelat Point, Afzal Khan's Tomb, and the heritage Shivkalin Village.

Included in: Tour 3 (Pratapgadh Darshan)
Tour Duration: 3 Hours 30 Minutes (24 km drive through scenic ghats)
Official Tour Fare: ₹1,600/- (4-Seater Black & Yellow Taxi)
Fort Guide
Category 5

Panchgani Plateaus & Strawberry Hubs

Asia's 2nd longest plateau, strawberry orchards, and theme parks.

Table Land (Asia's 2nd Longest Plateau)

Tour 4

A vast 95-acre flat volcanic laterite plateau at 4,500ft altitude. Famous for horse riding, Devil's Kitchen caves, and panoramic valley views.

Tour: Tour 4 (Panchgani Darshan - ₹1,200)
Duration: 3.00 Hours

Parsi Point & Krishna Valley

Tour 4

Breezy cliff overlooking the glittering waters of Dhom Dam and green hills of Krishna Valley. Perfect for family photos, children's park, and telescopes.

Tour: Tour 4 (Panchgani Darshan - ₹1,200)
Duration: 3.00 Hours

Mapro Garden (Gureghar)

Optional Add-on / Separate Drop

Famous strawberry park featuring fresh strawberry ice cream, wood-fired pizza, chocolate factory, and fruit syrup shopping.

Fare: ₹600 Drop / ₹800 Return (Separate union tariff / Extra on Tour 4)
Timings: 8:30 AM – 10:00 PM
Category 6

Tapola (Mini Kashmir) & Shivsagar Lake

Located 28 km downstream from Mahabaleshwar at the confluence of Koyna and Solshi rivers. The premier hub for speed boat rides, kayaking, water scooters, and scenic 90-km reservoir cruises.

Included in: Tour 6 (Tapola Darshan)
Tour Duration: 3 Hours 30 Minutes
Official Tour Fare: ₹1,450/- (4-Seater Black & Yellow Taxi)
Optional Add-on: Manghar Honey Village (India's 1st Village of Honey)
Tapola Guide
Category 7

Wai Heritage & Dhom Dam (Tour 5)

Discover "Dakshin Kashi" with over 100 ancient stone temples along the sacred Krishna river ghats. Features the monolithic Dholya Ganpati, Kashivishweshwar Temple, Dhom Dam reservoir, Nana Phadnavis Wada at Menavli, and Mapro Factory.

Included in: Tour 5 (Wai Darshan)
Tour Duration: 6 Hours
Official Tour Fare: ₹2,500/- (4-Seater Tourist Taxi)
Wai Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about visiting Mahabaleshwar attractions by taxi.

Which taxi tour covers Arthur's Seat and Kate's Point?

Both Arthur's Seat and Kate's Point (along with Old Mahabaleshwar Panchganga & Shiva temples, Echo Point, Elephant Head, and a strawberry garden) are included in Tour 1 (Mahabaleshwar Darshan). The union fare is ₹1,200/- for 3.5 hours in a 4-seater taxi.

Which tour includes Lingmala Waterfall and Sunset Point?

Lingmala Waterfall, Wilson Point, Lodwick Point, Venna Mini Dam, and Mumbai Point (Sunset) are covered together in Tour 2 (Mahabaleshwar Darshan - Waterfall & Sunset Circuit) for ₹1,200/- (3.5 hours).

Can I visit all top places of Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani in 1 day?

Yes! Our Tour 7 (Full Day Combo) is specifically designed to cover Tour 1 viewpoints (Arthur's Seat, Kate's Point, Temples) plus Tour 4 Panchgani points (Table Land, Parsi Point) and Mapro Garden in 9 hours for ₹3,000/-.

Does the taxi pick up directly from my hotel or resort?

Yes, we provide direct doorstep pickup and drop from all hotels, resorts, villas, and homestays across Mahabaleshwar, Panchgani, Bhilar, Bhose, and Metgutad with zero advance payment.

Are monument entry tickets and boating charges included in the taxi fare?

No. The taxi fare covers fuel, vehicle, and driver services for the entire tour route. Entry tickets, parking fees (₹30-₹50 per point), and boating rides are paid directly by guests at the counters for 100% transparency.
