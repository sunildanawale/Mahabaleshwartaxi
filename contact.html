Booking & Service Guidelines

Official Mahabaleshwar Taxi guidelines for sightseeing tours and outstation transfers.

Payment Policy

Zero advance required for local sightseeing tours. Pay the driver directly upon tour completion. Outstation transfers require a nominal token advance for fuel scheduling.

Pickup Location Policy

Official tour tariffs include doorstep pickup within Mahabaleshwar town. Except for Mahabaleshwar town, for pickups from any other location, nominal additional charges will apply based on distance.

Vehicle & Driver Safety

All cabs are commercial tourist permit vehicles driven by licensed local drivers registered with the Taxi Union. Taxis are arranged as per availability. Cabs are clean, well-maintained, and tested for mountain ghat roads.
