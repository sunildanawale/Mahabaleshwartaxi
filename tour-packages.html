Frequently Asked Questions

Are these per person or per taxi rates?

All our tour rates are per taxi, not per person. A standard sedan can comfortably seat up to 4 passengers.

Are entry fees and parking included?

No, the tour packages only cover the taxi fare and driver charges. Tolls, parking, and entry tickets to any monuments or parks are to be paid directly by the passenger.

Do I need to pay an advance to book a local tour?

No advance payment is required for local Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani sightseeing tours. You can pay the driver directly at the end of your trip.

Can we customize the tour itinerary?

Yes! We offer flexible itineraries. If you wish to spend more time at a specific point or skip some, just let the driver know. Extra charges may apply only if you exceed the standard duration or distance significantly.

What languages does the driver speak?

Our drivers are fluent in Marathi and Hindi, and can comfortably understand and speak basic English to assist you throughout your tour.

Tour Guidelines & Pricing Standards

Everything you need to know before booking your sightseeing taxi.

Viewpoint Halt & Delay

Each package has a fixed duration (e.g. 3.5 hrs for Tour 1). Extra waiting beyond the package duration is charged at approx ₹120 to ₹130 per hour.

Weekend & Peak Season

During weekends and peak holiday periods (Summer, Diwali, Christmas/New Year), a minimum of 2 tours or a Full Day Combo (Tour 7) is recommended to ensure cab availability.

Group Bookings (>4 Pax)

Mountain taxis are union-licensed 4-seater vehicles. For groups of 5 or more guests, multiple cabs are coordinated through the Mahabaleshwar Taxi Union.
