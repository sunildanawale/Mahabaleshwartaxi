Mapro Garden

A Delightful Culinary and Agricultural Experience in Mahabaleshwar

Home » Places to Visit » Mapro Garden
Back to Previous Page

A Paradise for Food and Nature Lovers

No trip to Mahabaleshwar is complete without a visit to the iconic Mapro Garden. Situated in the neighboring town of Panchgani, Mapro Garden is a sprawling park, factory, and culinary destination rolled into one. Established by Mahabaleshwar Products (Mapro), this attraction celebrates the region's rich agricultural heritage, primarily focusing on strawberries.

Mapro Garden is vastly popular among families, couples, and food enthusiasts. The premises feature lush gardens, a chocolate factory, a nursery, a children's play area, and an extensive retail outlet selling an array of fruit-based products like jams, crushes, squashes, and syrups. Visitors are often treated to free tasting counters, allowing you to sample the delicious products before purchasing.

The centerpiece of the Mapro experience is undoubtedly its massive open-air restaurant. Famous for its wood-fired pizzas, sandwiches, and the legendary Strawberry with Fresh Cream, dining at Mapro Garden is a must-do activity. The lively atmosphere, surrounded by beautiful flora, makes it a perfect stopover for lunch or an evening snack.

During the strawberry season, the Mapro Strawberry Festival is a major draw, featuring cultural performances, strawberry picking, and innovative strawberry-based dishes.

Quick Facts

  • Timings: 8:00 AM to 8:30 PM (Daily)
  • Entry Fee: Free Entry for all
  • Specialty: Wood-fired pizza, Strawberry cream, and fresh crushes
  • Taxi Fare: ₹600 Drop / ₹800 Return

What to Do at Mapro Garden

Shopping & Tasting

Explore the vast retail outlet offering jams, syrups, and chocolates. The free tasting counters are a hit, letting you try unique flavors before you buy souvenirs for home.

The Food Court

Indulge in delicious vegetarian food. The giant grilled sandwiches and fresh wood-fired pizzas are legendary, perfectly followed by massive bowls of strawberry cream.

Chocolate Factory & Nursery

Witness the mesmerizing process of chocolate making through viewing galleries. Afterward, take a peaceful stroll through the beautiful nursery showcasing exotic plants.

Planning Your Visit

Mapro Garden is located on the Mahabaleshwar-Panchgani road, making it an easy addition to a Panchgani sightseeing tour or a dedicated trip. It is highly recommended to allocate at least 2 to 3 hours for your visit to fully enjoy the food, shopping, and leisurely environment without rushing.

The best time to visit for the quintessential strawberry experience is between December and April, which aligns with the local strawberry harvesting season. However, Mapro Garden remains an excellent destination year-round, offering seasonal fruit creams like Mulberry and Mango during other months.

Be prepared for large crowds, especially during lunchtime on weekends. Finding parking can be notoriously difficult. Booking a taxi with us ensures a hassle-free trip; our drivers handle the parking and wait for you while you enjoy your time inside.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Mapro Garden from Mahabaleshwar?

Mapro Garden is located about 11 kilometers from the main Mahabaleshwar market, towards Panchgani. It takes approximately 20-30 minutes by taxi depending on traffic.

Is the food at Mapro Garden purely vegetarian?

Yes, the food served at the Mapro Garden restaurant is 100% vegetarian. They offer a wide variety of delicious fast food and desserts.

Can we pick strawberries at Mapro Garden?

During the strawberry season (Dec-April), Mapro occasionally allows farm visits for picking, but many private farms nearby also offer this experience. You can buy fresh, pre-packed strawberries at Mapro anytime during the season.

Are pets allowed inside Mapro Garden?

Generally, pets are not allowed inside the main restaurant and shopping areas of Mapro Garden due to hygiene and crowd control reasons.
