A Journey into Maratha History

Pratapgad Fort stands as a monumental testament to the valor and strategic brilliance of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. Built in 1656 by Moropant Trimbak Pingle under Shivaji Maharaj's guidance, this hill fortress is situated 22 km west of Mahabaleshwar, standing proud at an elevation of over 1,000 meters above sea level.

The fort is famous across Indian history as the site of the legendary Battle of Pratapgad between Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and Afzal Khan, commander of the Bijapur Sultanate. Today, visitors can explore the upper and lower forts, the sacred Bhavani Temple, the tomb of Afzal Khan, and enjoy panoramic 360-degree views of the lush Konkan valleys.

Fort Trek & Walking Guide

Exploring the full fort involves climbing approximately 450 stone steps across the upper and lower ramparts.

What to See at the Fort

Bhavani Temple

The fort houses a renowned temple dedicated to Goddess Bhavani, established by Shivaji Maharaj. It features a beautiful idol made of pure Shaligram stone.

Shivaji Maharaj Statue

An imposing 17-foot tall equestrian bronze statue of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj stands proudly at the summit, inaugurated by Jawaharlal Nehru in 1957.

Kadelot Point

The western edge of the fort. Historically used for executing traitors by pushing them off the cliff, it now offers dramatic views of the deep ravines.

Trekking & Travel Tips

  • Difficulty: Moderate. The climb involves around 450 stone steps.
  • Duration: It takes about 30-45 minutes to climb up, depending on your fitness level.
  • Attire: Wear comfortable cotton clothes and sturdy sports shoes or trekking shoes with good grip. Avoid heels.
  • Hydration: Carry a water bottle, though local vendors sell water and nimbu pani along the stairs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Pratapgad Fort trek suitable for elderly people?

The climb involves around 450 steps which can be strenuous. While there are resting spots, it may not be suitable for elderly people with knee problems or heart conditions. Palkhi (palanquin) services are occasionally available but better confirmed locally.

Are there food options available at the fort?

Yes, there are small local stalls on the way up and near the base parking offering snacks, tea, buttermilk, and traditional Maharashtrian thalis.

How much does the taxi tour cost?

Our Pratapgad Tour (Tour 3) costs ₹1600 for a 4-seater taxi. This covers the return journey and waiting time.

What is the best time of day to visit?

Early morning is the best time to visit to avoid the midday sun during the climb. Alternatively, late afternoon offers pleasant weather and great sunset views.
