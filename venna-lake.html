A Must-Visit Destination in Mahabaleshwar

Constructed in 1842 by Shri Appasaheb Maharaj, the ruler of Satara, Venna Lake stands as one of the most prominent tourist attractions in Mahabaleshwar. Spread across 28 acres with a periphery of 7-8 km, this stunning artificial lake is surrounded by lush green trees on all sides, offering a serene escape for travelers seeking both relaxation and recreation. Whether you are traveling with family, friends, or as a couple, Venna Lake provides an idyllic setting that appeals to all age groups.

The shimmering waters reflect the rich greenery of the Sahyadri mountains, creating picturesque views especially during the early mornings and late afternoons. Visitors often find themselves enchanted by the cool breeze and the gentle rippling of the water, making it the perfect spot to unwind after a long day of sightseeing around Mahabaleshwar.

Apart from the natural beauty, Venna Lake is heavily commercialized in a way that caters directly to tourists. The bustling atmosphere is a stark contrast to the quiet forested areas of Mahabaleshwar, yet it maintains its unique charm. The lakeside promenade is perfect for leisurely strolls, taking in the sights and sounds of joyful vacationers.

  • Timings: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM (Daily)
  • Entry Fee: Free (Activities are chargeable)

Activities at Venna Lake

Boating

The main attraction here is boating. Choose between traditional row boats navigated by local boatmen, or opt for pedal boats if you prefer to navigate the waters yourself. Life jackets are provided for safety, ensuring a worry-free experience on the water.

Horse Riding

For children and adventure enthusiasts, horse riding is a popular activity around the lake's periphery. Local guides offer short rides along designated paths, providing a fun and memorable experience, especially for young visitors.

Street Food

The lakeside is lined with numerous food stalls offering local Maharashtrian snacks. Indulge in hot corn (bhutta), fresh strawberry cream, mulberries, bhel puri, and pav bhaji. The food scene here is an integral part of the Venna Lake experience.

More About The Experience

Spending an evening at Venna Lake is a quintessential Mahabaleshwar experience. As the sun begins to set, the entire area comes alive with colorful lights, bustling tourists, and the mouth-watering aroma of street food. It's highly recommended to combine your visit to Venna Lake with other nearby viewpoints, but allocate at least 2-3 hours here to truly enjoy everything it has to offer.

During peak seasons (Diwali, Christmas, and summer holidays), the boating queues can get quite long. It's advisable to arrive early in the morning or mid-afternoon to avoid the longest waits. Our taxi service ensures you reach safely and conveniently, navigating the often crowded parking areas effortlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Venna Lake?

The best time to visit Venna Lake is in the late afternoon, around 4:00 PM. This allows you to enjoy boating in pleasant weather and catch a beautiful sunset. Early mornings are also great for a quieter, more peaceful experience with misty surroundings.

Is parking available at Venna Lake?

Yes, there is a designated parking area near the lake, but it gets extremely crowded during weekends and peak tourist seasons. Booking a local taxi with us eliminates the hassle of finding a parking spot.

Are food stalls open throughout the day?

Most food stalls open around 9:00 AM and stay open until 9:00 PM or later, especially during peak seasons. You can easily find snacks and meals at any time during your visit.

Is boating safe for young children?

Yes, boating is generally safe. Life jackets are provided and must be worn. The row boats are operated by experienced local oarsmen who ensure a smooth ride across the lake.
