Official Tour 5

Discover the Temple Heritage of "Dakshin Kashi"

Wai is an ancient spiritual town set along the sacred Krishna river ghats, renowned for over 100 historical temples, Maratha-era architecture, and Bollywood movie backdrops (Swades, Chennai Express). Combined with the tranquil waters of Dhom Dam and the Mapro Food Park, this is one of our most popular full half-day circuits.

  • Duration: 6 Hours
  • Fare: ₹2,500/- (Registered Union Rate for 4-Seater Taxi)
  • Vehicle: Black & Yellow Tourist Taxi (4-Seater)
  • Pickup: Direct from your hotel in Mahabaleshwar / Panchgani
Itinerary Details

Attractions Covered in Tour 5

Complete sightseeing list as per the official Mahabaleshwar Taxi Union Tariff Card.

Sydney Point

Enjoy spectacular aerial views of the Krishna Valley, Dhom Dam, and Pandavgad Fort from the edge of Panchgani plateau.

Shree Ganesh & Kashivishweshwar

Visit the famous monolithic 'Dholya Ganpati' temple built in 1762 and the adjacent ancient stone Shiva temple on the river ghat.

Dhom Dam Backwaters

A massive gravity dam on the Krishna river offering cool mountain breezes, speed boating, and pristine nature views.

Nana Phadnavis Wada (Menavli)

Explore the classic Maratha heritage mansion and historic riverside bell tower, famously featured in Bollywood cinema.

Mapro Factory (Shendurjane)

Tour the flagship Mapro manufacturing facility with strawberry syrups, chocolates, jams, and delicious food court delights.
