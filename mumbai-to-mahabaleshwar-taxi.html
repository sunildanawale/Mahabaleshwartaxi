Mumbai Pickup & Expressway Route Guide

Travel effortlessly from Mumbai's coastal plains to the invigorating climate of Mahabaleshwar. Our professional mountain drivers ensure a smooth 260 km drive via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.

  • Mumbai Airport (BOM): Direct pickup at Terminal 1 (Domestic) or Terminal 2 (International) arrival exits.
  • South Mumbai & Atal Setu: Fast transit via MTHL Atal Setu or Eastern Freeway to Expressway.
  • Suburbs & Thane: Pickup from Bandra, Andheri, Borivali, Powai, Thane, Vashi, and Kharghar.
  • Total Journey: Approx 260 km, typically 5.5 to 6.5 hours door-to-door.

Recommended Expressway Food Plazas

The Mumbai-Pune Expressway features world-class food plazas at Khalapur and Talegaon offering McDonald's, Starbucks, Shivraj, and clean washroom facilities. A quick 30-minute break keeps the journey pleasant for families and seniors.

Mumbai to Mahabaleshwar Travel FAQs

What is the best route from South Mumbai using Atal Setu (MTHL)?

From South Mumbai, taking the Atal Setu (MTHL) from Sewri to Chirle bypasses Chembur and Vashi traffic completely, cutting 35-40 minutes off the drive to the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.

Can I schedule a late night or early morning pickup from Mumbai Airport?

Yes, we offer 24/7 airport pickups. Sharing your flight number allows the assigned driver to position the cab at BOM before you land.

Do we drive through Pune city on this route?

No, we take the dedicated Dehu Road-Katraj expressway bypass, completely avoiding Pune city traffic and heading straight toward Shirwal and Wai.

Is advance payment required for Mumbai pickups?

A nominal fuel commitment advance is requested for outstation pickups originating in Mumbai, with the remaining balance payable directly to the driver at destination.
