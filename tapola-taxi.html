Official Tour 6

Escape to the "Mini Kashmir" of Maharashtra

Tapola is a picturesque rustic village nestled 28 km downstream of Mahabaleshwar at the vast reservoir of the Koyna Dam (Shivsagar Lake). The drive itself is an enchanting experience, cutting through dense evergreen Sahyadri forests with panoramic valley overlooks.

  • Duration: 3 Hours 30 Minutes
  • Fare: ₹1,450/- (Registered Union Rate for 4-Seater Taxi)
  • Vehicle: Black & Yellow Tourist Taxi (4-Seater)
  • Pickup: Direct from your hotel lobby in Mahabaleshwar
Itinerary Details

Attractions Covered in Tour 6

Complete sightseeing list as per the official Mahabaleshwar Taxi Union Tariff Card.

Mini Kashmir Landscape

Enjoy the breathtaking view of the tranquil backwaters surrounded by lush green hills, earning Tapola its famous nickname "Mini Kashmir".

Shivsagar Water Sports

Take thrilling speed boat rides, water scooters, or peaceful family motorboat cruises across the 90 km long reservoir.

Shivsagar Darshan

Visit scenic vantage points overlooking the confluence of the Koyna and Solshi rivers with majestic mountain vistas.

Extra Cost Optional Attraction:

Honey Village (Manghar): India's first official "Village of Honey", where local beekeepers produce pure natural forest honey. Available at a nominal additional charge upon request.

The fixed rate is ₹1,450/- per 4-seater black-and-yellow tourist taxi as authorized by the Mahabaleshwar Taxi Union. This includes both the forward journey, return journey, and waiting time.

Zero advance payment! You can confirm your booking directly on WhatsApp or phone call and pay the driver in cash or via UPI after completing your tour.
