Bombay Point (Sunset Point)

The Most Famous Spot to Watch the Sun Go Down in Mahabaleshwar

Home » Places to Visit » Bombay Point (Sunset Point)
Back to Previous Page

A Breathtaking End to Your Day

Bombay Point, widely known as the primary Sunset Point of Mahabaleshwar, is undeniably one of the most visited locations in the hill station. Earning its name because it is situated on the old road that once led directly to Bombay (now Mumbai), this viewpoint offers an expansive, unobstructed view of the sun dipping below the horizon.

As evening approaches, the sky transforms into a spectacular canvas of deep reds, vibrant oranges, and soft purples. The view looks out over the vast expanse of the Pratapgad Fort valley and the distant Makrandgarh mountain. The sheer cliff drop and the sprawling valleys below create a dramatic and unforgettable setting for sunset viewing.

Unlike some of the quieter points in Mahabaleshwar, Bombay Point is known for its lively and bustling atmosphere. Hundreds of tourists gather here every evening to bid farewell to the day. The area is spacious, featuring a large open area where visitors can stroll, relax, and find the perfect spot to capture photographs of the descending sun.

The combination of natural beauty, historical significance, and a festive atmosphere makes Bombay Point an essential part of any Mahabaleshwar itinerary.

Quick Facts

  • Best Time: 5:00 PM to 6:30 PM (Sunset)
  • Entry Fee: Free
  • Key Views: Pratapgad Fort, Makrandgarh, Sahyadri Sunset
  • Taxi Tour: Included in Tour 2 (Waterfall & Sunset - ₹1,200)

What to Expect at Bombay Point

Spectacular Sunsets

The vast horizon offers a clear and uninterrupted view of the sunset. The changing colors of the sky contrasting with the dark silhouettes of the Sahyadri ranges create a mesmerizing visual treat.

Lively Atmosphere

Join the crowd of enthusiastic travelers. It's a great place to people-watch, enjoy local snacks from roaming vendors, and soak in the energetic vibe of a popular tourist destination.

Activities & Games

The large flat plateau area features horse riding for children and various local carnival-style games, making it an excellent spot for family entertainment while waiting for the sun to set.

Tips for the Best Experience

Because Bombay Point is the most popular sunset spot in Mahabaleshwar, it can get exceptionally crowded, particularly on weekends and during peak tourist seasons (Summer and Diwali holidays). We strongly advise reaching the point at least 45 minutes to an hour before the scheduled sunset time.

Arriving early not only ensures you secure a prime viewing location right at the edge of the railing but also allows you to enjoy the area before the major crowds descend. Parking can become a significant challenge as evening approaches. By booking a taxi with us, you avoid the stress of navigating the congested roads and finding a parking spot. Our drivers know the best drop-off points and will wait for you nearby while you enjoy the sunset.

Don't forget to bring your camera! The lighting just before the sunset-the golden hour-is perfect for taking portraits and sweeping landscape shots.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it called Bombay Point?

It is called Bombay Point because it is situated on the old highway that historically connected Mahabaleshwar to Bombay (Mumbai). The road is no longer the primary route, but the name stuck.

How far is Bombay Point from the main market?

Bombay Point is approximately 3 to 4 kilometers from the main Mahabaleshwar market. It's a short 15-minute drive, though traffic during sunset hours might increase travel time.

Are there any food options at Bombay Point?

Yes, numerous local vendors roam the area and set up small stalls selling street food like chana jor garam, roasted corn, peanuts, and local berries during the season.

Is Bombay Point wheelchair accessible?

The main viewing area is a large, mostly flat plateau, making it reasonably accessible for wheelchairs, although some areas closer to the edge might have uneven terrain.
