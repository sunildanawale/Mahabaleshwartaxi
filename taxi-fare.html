Pricing & Fare FAQs

Everything you need to know about how our pricing works.

Are these fares per person or per taxi?

All rates mentioned on this page are per taxi, not per person. A 4-seater taxi can comfortably seat up to 4 passengers plus the driver. The cost remains the same whether there is 1 passenger or 4.

Are tolls, parking, and entry fees included in the tour price?

No. The tour packages cover only the cost of the taxi and the driver's service. Any toll taxes, parking fees at tourist points (usually ₹30-₹50 per point), and your personal entry tickets to monuments or parks are to be paid directly by you at the counters. This ensures absolute transparency.

Do I need to pay for the driver's food (Bhatta)?

For local Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani sightseeing tours (like the ₹3000 Full Day combo), no driver bhatta is required. The driver takes care of his own meals. However, for outstation trips spanning multiple days, a standard driver allowance of ₹300 per day is applicable.

Do I have to pay an advance to book?

For local sightseeing tours, we require zero advance payment. You can book simply by calling or sending a WhatsApp message, and you pay the full amount directly to the driver at the end of the tour. For outstation drops to Mumbai or Pune, a small token advance may be requested.

Is the price fixed, or can we negotiate?

We operate strictly on the official rate card set by the Mahabaleshwar Taxi Union. Our prices are fixed to ensure fairness to all customers and drivers alike. By booking direct, you are already getting the lowest possible union rate without any middleman commission.

Why is the AC turned off on the Ghats?

This is a standard safety protocol for all hill station taxis. The steep inclines of Mahabaleshwar and Pratapgad put immense strain on the engine. Turning off the AC during steep climbs prevents engine overheating and ensures a safe, uninterrupted journey. The AC is turned back on as soon as the road levels out.
