Pune Pickup & Highway Route Guide

Starting your journey from Pune to the cool Sahyadri hills of Mahabaleshwar is smooth and scenic with Aryan Taxi Mahabaleshwar. We provide door-to-door cab pickups from any location across Pune and transfer you straight to your resort lobby.

  • Pune Airport (PNQ) Pickup: Direct flight arrival gate meeting. Flight delay tracking included at no extra charge.
  • Pune Junction Railway Station: Pickup at Platform 1 main porch or Raja Bahadur Mill Road entrance.
  • IT Parks & Suburbs: Hinjewadi (Phases 1, 2, 3), Wakad, Baner, Aundh, Kothrud, Magarpatta, Kharadi, and Viman Nagar.
  • Highway Route: 120 km via NH48, new Khambatki tunnel bypass, Surur, Wai, and scenic Pasarni Ghat ascent (~3.5 Hours).

Highway Stops & New Khambatki Tunnel

The Pune-Bengaluru NH48 features popular food plazas near Shirwal and Surur for Misal Pav and tea. The newly opened Khambatki Ghat bypass tunnel eliminates the historic truck traffic jam on the hill climb, saving 20-30 minutes before you ascend Pasarni Ghat into Panchgani.

Pune to Mahabaleshwar Travel FAQs

What happens if my flight into Pune Airport is delayed?

We track your flight arrival timing in real time. If your flight is delayed, our driver adjusts the pickup schedule automatically with zero waiting penalty or extra fee.

What is the best departure time from Pune to avoid traffic?

Departing between 6:00 AM and 8:00 AM allows you to clear the Katraj bypass before morning city rush hour. Post-lunch departures between 1:30 PM and 3:00 PM are also very smooth.

Can we pause at Mapro Garden on the way up to our hotel?

Yes! Mapro Garden is located directly along the main Panchgani-Mahabaleshwar road. Your driver can pause for 30-45 minutes for strawberry snacks before delivering you to your resort.

How much luggage can a 4-seater taxi accommodate?

A standard 4-seater tourist taxi comfortably accommodates 2 large trolley suitcases and 2 medium backpacks in the dedicated boot space.
