Why It's the Queen of Points

Arthur's Seat Point is arguably the most famous viewpoint in Mahabaleshwar. It is named after Sir Arthur Mallet, who used to sit here and stare into the Savitri valley, longing for his wife and children who drowned in the Savitri river below.

The viewpoint is unique because it clearly distinguishes the geological contrast between the Konkan coast and the Deccan plateau. The fascinating stratified laterite rocks and the dramatic 1,470m drop into the valley make it a mesmerizing sight.

Highlights Around Arthur's Seat

The Floating Legend

Due to strong updrafts of air pressure from the deep valley, light objects like an empty bottle cap thrown into the valley are known to float back up! It's a fun test of the unique wind currents here.

Echo Point

Located just before Arthur's Seat, this point reverberates your voice across the valley. It's a favorite among kids and adults alike.

Tiger Spring

A small natural spring of pure water situated a short walk down from the main point. Legend says tigers used to come here to drink.

Window Point

Named for the unique window-like rock formation that offers a framed view of the valley below. Great for photography.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much walking is required?

From the taxi parking area, you will need to walk for about 10-15 minutes (approx 500 meters) on a paved pathway to reach the main viewing deck. The walk includes a few steps but is generally easy.

Is it safe for children?

Yes, the main viewing platforms have strong iron railings for safety. However, always keep children accompanied and do not let them climb on the railings.

What is the best time to visit Arthur's Seat?

Early morning around 9 AM or late afternoon are the best times. It gets very crowded between 11 AM and 3 PM. During monsoons, the point is often covered in dense fog with zero visibility, though the misty ambiance is magical.

Are food and washrooms available?

Yes, near the parking lot, there are numerous stalls selling sweet corn, maggi, tea, and strawberries. Basic public washroom facilities are also available near the parking.
