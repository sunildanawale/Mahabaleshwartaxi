Top Destinations

Explore the magic of the Sahyadri mountains.

Most Popular

Arthur's Seat

Often called the "Queen of all points", offering spectacular views of the deep Savitri Valley. Known for the anti-gravitational phenomenon where light items thrown down float up.

Venna Lake

A serene man-made lake built by Shri Appasaheb Maharaj in 1842. Perfect for paddle or row boating, horse riding, and enjoying fresh strawberries on the banks.

Kate's Point & Elephant's Head

Famous for the natural rock formation resembling an elephant's head and trunk. Offers panoramic views of the Dhom Dam and the Krishna Valley.

Lingmala Waterfall

A magnificent waterfall that is best visited during or right after the monsoon. The trek to the main falls offers lush greenery and a cool misty breeze.

Panchganga Temple

Located in Old Mahabaleshwar, this ancient temple is believed to be the origin point of five rivers: Krishna, Venna, Savitri, Koyna, and Gayatri.

Bombay Point (Sunset)

The most popular spot in Mahabaleshwar to witness the spectacular sunset over the valleys. It gets crowded, so our drivers will make sure you arrive early for the best view.

Mapro Garden

A very popular tourist attraction known for its strawberry produce, delicious fruit salads, wood-fired pizzas, and scenic garden setting.

Table Land (Panchgani)

Asia's second-largest mountain plateau. Famous for horse rides, sunset views, and as a filming location for numerous Bollywood movies.

Pratapgad Fort

A historic mountain fort built by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. Explore its majestic bastions, Bhavani temple, and incredible valley views.

Tapola (Mini Kashmir)

Known as the 'Mini Kashmir of the West', Tapola offers serene boating experiences on the massive Shivsagar Lake surrounded by lush green mountains.

Dhom Dam (Wai)

A spectacular earthfill and gravity dam on the Krishna river. A peaceful location perfect for camping, boating, and enjoying stunning mountain reflections.

Parsi Point (Panchgani)

A popular viewpoint in Panchgani offering breathtaking panoramic views of the Krishna River valley and the Dhom Dam backwaters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much time is needed for the Arthur's Seat tour?

The standard Old Mahabaleshwar and Arthur's Seat tour covers around 7 points and usually takes 2.5 to 3 hours to complete comfortably.

Is there an entry fee for these sightseeing points?

Most viewpoints are free to visit, though you may need to pay nominal parking fees for the taxi (usually ₹20-₹50) or a local entry tax at the town toll naka.

What is the best time to start local sightseeing?

We recommend starting around 9:30 AM to beat the crowds at popular spots like Arthur's Seat and to avoid the harsh afternoon sun.

Is the taxi fare fixed for local tours?

Yes, local tour packages have fixed, union-approved rates. For example, the Mahabaleshwar local darshan starts at ₹1,200.

Can we customize our sightseeing route?

Absolutely! Since we are independent, owner-operated taxis, we can tailor the route to your preferences. Just tell the driver what you want to skip or spend more time on.
