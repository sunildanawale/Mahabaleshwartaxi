Frequently Asked Questions

Where will the driver meet me at Pune/Mumbai Airport?

Our driver will wait near the arrivals exit gate with your name or will call you as soon as your flight lands to coordinate a convenient pickup point.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We monitor flight statuses. Please inform us via WhatsApp if you know of a significant delay, but our drivers will wait for you regardless.

Do you charge extra for night airport pickups?

Standard fares apply, but a small night driving allowance (usually ₹300–₹500) may be added for pickups between 11 PM and 5 AM. Please confirm at the time of booking.

Can you pick us up from Satara Railway Station?

Yes, Satara Railway Station is the nearest major railhead. A pickup from Satara to Mahabaleshwar costs approximately ₹2,300 + Toll in a Non-AC 4-seater taxi.

Is advance booking necessary?

Yes, we strongly recommend booking your airport transfer at least 24 hours in advance to secure timely vehicle coordination.
