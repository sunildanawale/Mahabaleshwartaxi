The Charm of Panchgani

Panchgani, often referred to as the twin city of Mahabaleshwar, is famous for its unpolluted air, magnificent valleys, and lush green plateaus. Whether you want to enjoy a serene sunset, explore historical caves, or simply take in the expansive views from Asia's second-largest plateau, Panchgani has it all.

Our dedicated Panchgani Taxi Tour covers all major attractions efficiently. With fares ranging from ₹1,200 to ₹2,500 depending on your pickup location and vehicle type, you can explore comfortably without any hassle.

Top Attractions in Panchgani

What you'll see on this tour

Table Land

Asia's second-largest mountain plateau. Enjoy horse riding, panoramic views of the entire region, and explore the spacious laterite rock formations.

Parsi Point

Offers spectacular views of the Krishna River valley and Dhom Dam backwaters. A perfect spot to relax and witness a breathtaking sunset.

Sydney Point

Situated on a hillock facing the Krishna Valley, Sydney Point provides excellent views of the Dhom Dam, Pandavgad, and Mandhardeo.

Devil's Kitchen

Located south of Table Land, myth says the Pandavas of the Mahabharata stayed here and used these caves for cooking during their exile.

Bhilar Waterfall

A stunning seasonal waterfall visible during the monsoons. Located on the way to Panchgani, it is a great photography stop.

Mapro Garden

While technically between Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani, no trip is complete without stopping here for fresh strawberries, shakes, and jams.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much time is required for Panchgani sightseeing?

A typical Panchgani sightseeing tour takes about 3 to 4 hours. If you spend more time at Mapro Garden or go horse riding at Table Land, it might take a bit longer.

Is Table Land open throughout the year?

Yes, Table Land is open all year round. However, the best time to visit is during the post-monsoon or winter season when the weather is pleasant. During heavy monsoons, it can get very slippery and foggy.

Are entry fees included in the taxi fare?

No, the taxi fare only covers transportation. Any entry fees, parking fees at specific points, or activity charges (like horse riding) are to be paid directly by the customer.

Can we combine Panchgani and Mahabaleshwar sightseeing in one day?

Yes, if you start early, you can cover major points of both Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani in a single day. However, it will be a bit rushed. We recommend dedicating half a day to each for a relaxed experience.
